## Brand & Style

This design system serves as an authoritative institutional framework for academic and religious governance, specifically tailored for the national allocation, quota modeling, and performance evaluation of moral teaching monks across Thai educational jurisdictions.

The personality balances bureaucratic solemnity with progressive civic clarity:
- **Authoritative & Reverent:** Deep civic navies ground the experience in official institutional credibility, tempered by sacred ochre and saffron accents that respect Buddhist clerical traditions without becoming decorative or archaic.
- **Analytical & Precise:** Designed for dense statistical models, ecclesiastical quota thresholds, and multi-tier institutional approvals. Every metric, headcount, and status shift is rendered with unambiguous clarity.
- **Accessible & Transparent:** Prioritizes high legibility, uncompromised structural grid discipline, and strict visual hierarchies to facilitate rapid auditing by ministerial directors, educational district officers, and monastic supervisors.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Modern Civic Tech** with **Structured Institutional Minimalism**: crisp low-contrast borders, refined atmospheric surface layers, disciplined micro-spacing, and data-dense tabular ergonomics.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects civic responsibility and ecclesiastical academic heritage. The foundational layers consist of clean, low-fatigue slate backgrounds, high-contrast typography, and purposeful state indicators.

### Key Palettes
- **Primary Navy (`#1B365D`) & Deep Royal Base (`#0F2942`):** Communicates sovereign governance, formal institutional mandate, and data integrity. Used for top-level navigation, structural headers, high-emphasis action buttons, and dominant data points.
- **Sacred Saffron / Dignified Ochre (`#B45309` primary, `#D97706` interactive, `#F59E0B` highlight):** Draws inspiration from traditional monastic robes and institutional Buddhist seals. Deployed strategically for allocation quota callouts, monk identification markers, active evaluation tags, and warning conditions.
- **Slate Canvas (`#F8FAFC` base background, `#F1F5F9` sub-canvas & table headers, `#FFFFFF` card surfaces):** Prevents eye strain during prolonged quota planning and audit review cycles.
- **Structural Lines (`#E2E8F0` standard, `#CBD5E1` emphasis):** Delivers clean structural containment for complex data matrices.

### Semantic & Monastic State Rules
- **Active Clergy / Approved Allocation:** Emerald Green (`#059669` fill, `#ECFDF5` container, `#065F46` label).
- **Pending Review / Quota Warning:** Saffron Ochre (`#D97706` fill, `#FFFBEB` container, `#92400E` label).
- **Vacant Quota / Disrobed / Deficit:** Rose Red (`#DC2626` fill, `#FEF2F2` container, `#991B1B` label).
- **Administrative Transfer / Reassignment:** Indigo Blue (`#2563EB` fill, `#EFF6FF` container, `#1E40AF` label).
- **Deceased / Emeritus Honor Roll:** Slate Charcoal (`#475569` fill, `#F1F5F9` container, `#0F172A` label).

## Typography

The typography stack relies on clean, high-legibility sans-serif metrics optimized for bilingual institutional environments (Latin and Thai). When deployed in production environments, the system pairs standard high-clarity font weights (`400`, `500`, `600`, `700`) with proportional tabular lining figures to ensure financial and headcount figures align reliably across vertical tables.

- **Headlines & Section Titles:** Emphasize formal structural hierarchy using condensed vertical line-heights and slight negative tracking.
- **Numbers & Quota Statistics:** The dedicated `metric-num` role ensures values such as assigned monk headcount, pedagogical evaluations, and budget lines stand out prominently across executive dashboards.
- **Data Densities:** Dense data tables exclusively utilize `body-sm` and `label-md` with `line-height: 1.35` to maximize row real estate without sacrificing vertical text scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a 12-column responsive fluid grid structured within a strict maximum container width of `1440px`.

### Grid System & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) screen edge margins. Sidebar is anchored at a fixed width of `280px` for hierarchical departmental navigation (Region > Province > District > Temple / School).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters. The hierarchical sidebar collapses into an overlay drawer.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) horizontal gutters. Multi-tier approval tables reflow into stacked assessment record cards.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spatial grouping adheres to strict hierarchical containment:
- **Intra-component (2px – 8px):** Spacing between monk ecclesiastical titles and names, status dots, and indicator badges.
- **Inter-field (12px – 16px):** Form fields within evaluation matrices, input labels, and instructional microcopy.
- **Structural Grouping (24px – 32px):** Spacing separating statistical overview cards, interactive quota simulation controls, and historical evaluation review logs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal surfaces and low-contrast outlines** reinforced by restrained ambient shadows. This prevents distracting skeuomorphic clutter and maintains an authoritative civic presence.

### Depth Tiers
1. **Base Layer (Elevation 0 - `#F8FAFC`):** Application canvas underpinning all panels, sidebars, and workspaces.
2. **Surface Layer (Elevation 1 - `#FFFFFF`):** High-density cards, analytical panels, and calculation workbenches. Supported by a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient micro-shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
3. **Elevated Elements (Elevation 2):** Quota scenario simulation sliders, filter action groups, and active hover cards:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
4. **Modal Dialogs & Overlays (Elevation 3):** Dual-level institutional approval sign-offs, credential inspectors, and quota transfer modals:
   - `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
   - Underpinned by an authoritative slate backdrop overlay: `rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.48)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a calibrated roundedness level (`roundedness: 2`) to humanize the civil service experience while preserving organizational discipline.

- **Cards & Data Modules:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px on outer boundaries or 1rem / 16px on internal cards) providing distinct modular containment for quota tables, scoring rubrics, and demographic maps.
- **Input Controls & Action Buttons:** `rounded-md` (0.375rem / 6px to 0.5rem / 8px) offering structured visual contrast against expansive background surfaces.
- **Status Tags & Clerical Badges:** Fully rounded pill format (`rounded-full`) to immediately differentiate transactional metadata from interactive field elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Civic Action:** Deep Navy fill (`#1B365D`), text `#FFFFFF`, border none, 8px padding vertical, 16px horizontal. Hover transitions to `#0F2942`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#1B365D`.
- **Secondary / Allocation Action:** Sacred Ochre fill (`#B45309`), text `#FFFFFF`. Used strictly for quota adjustments, final verification submissions, and formal stamp approvals.
- **Outline / Neutral:** Slate border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), surface transparent or `#FFFFFF`, text `#334155`. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Monastic Status Badges
- Compact height (24px to 28px) with `rounded-full` contours and semi-bold 11px uppercase/tabular labels.
- **Active Monk Badge:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, framed by `#A7F3D0` border with a 6px pulsing emerald indicator dot.
- **Vacant Allocation Badge:** `#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text, framed by `#FECACA` border with a static red dot.
- **Academic Degree Chip (e.g., Nak Tham Ek, Pali VI):** `#F1F5F9` background, `#1E293B` text, `1px solid #CBD5E1`.

### Data-Dense Evaluation Tables
- Alternate row striping using `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`.
- Header row styled in `#F1F5F9` with a subtle bottom border (`1px solid #CBD5E1`), text rendered in `label-sm` color `#475569`.
- Column alignments: Monk ecclesiastical name and designated school are left-aligned; evaluation scores and assigned quotas are right-aligned with fixed tabular spacing; status tags are centered.

### Dual-Level Approval Stepper
- Horizontal civic pipeline tracking monastic supervisory endorsement (Ecclesiastical Dean / Chao Khana) alongside secular governmental verification (Educational Service Area Office).
- Active step: Royal Navy ring with Saffron fill.
- Approved step: Emerald circle containing white check icon.
- Pending step: Inset slate border with muted text.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height: 40px for desktop workflows.
- Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1` around a `#FFFFFF` surface.
- Focus State: Border color shifts to `#1B365D` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.12)`).
